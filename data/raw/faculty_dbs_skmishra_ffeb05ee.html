Dr. Shravan Kumar Mishra

Dr. Shravan Kumar Mishra

Associate Professor

Department of Biological Sciences

📍 AB2 3F3

✉️ skmishra@iisermohali.ac.in
  • Research
  • Career Highlights
  • Awards & Recognitions
  • Publications

Research Area

Molecular Cell Biology

Research Interests

Alternative Splicing mechanisms; Assembly of splicing factories through Liquid-Liquid Phase Separation (LLPS); Evolution and function of ubiquitin-like proteins; Pathways of protein degradation; Post-translational modifications in nutrient assimilation; Approaches: Molecular Cell Biology, Biochemistry and Genetics studies in the budding yeast Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the fission yeast Schizosaccharomyces pombe and cultured mammalian cells.

Since 2018 : Associate Professor, Department of Biological Sciences, IISER Mohali, India

2012 - 2018 : Assistant Professor, Department of Biological Sciences, IISER Mohali, India

2004 - 2012 : Post-Doc, Max Planck Institute of Biochemistry, Martinsried/Munich, Germany

1999 - 2004 : PhD, Biocentre, J.W. Goethe University of Frankfurt, Germany

1997 - 1999 : M.Sc., Biotechnology, MS University of Baroda, India

1995 - 1997 : B.Sc., Microbiology and Biochemistry, KJ Somaiya college, University of Mumbai, , India

Associate Dean Academics, 2023-2025

Indo-French CEFIPRA joint project, 2025-ongoing

Wellcome/DBT India Alliance Intermediate Fellow, 2019-2024

Head of the Max Planck Partner Group at IISER Mohali, 2013-2018

Max Planck Junior Research award for outstanding research, 2011

PhD Graduate Program of Goethe University of Frankfurt, 2000-2002
