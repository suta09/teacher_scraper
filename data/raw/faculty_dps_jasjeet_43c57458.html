Research Area

Astrophysics

Research Interests

Cosmology, Galaxy Formation, Gravitational Lensing, Gravitational clustering in an expanding universe.  My research over the last decade has covered these areas while working on specific problems.

 

2022 - 2027 : Editor, New Astronomy

2021 - 2026 : Associate Editor, Resonance

2023 - 2025 : Chairperson, Scientific Organisation Committee, Astronomical Society of India

1999 - 2010 : Faculty member, Harish-Chandra Research Institute, Allahabad, India

1996 - 1998 : Post-doctoral research associate, Institute of Astronomy, University of Cambridge, UK

1998 - 1999 : Post-doctoral research associate, Harvard-Smithsonian Centre for Astrophysics, USA

1992 - 1996 : PhD, IUCAA, Pune, India

1990 - 1992 : M.Sc. Physics, University of Delhi

1986 - 1990 : B.Sc.(H) Physics, University of Delhi

Member of the drafting group for the Astronomy and Astrophysics component of the Mega Science Vision-2035 Exercise for India instituted by the office of the Principal Scientific Advisor in 2022.

Editor for Research in Astronomy and Astrophysics during 2009-2013

Member of academic committee, International Olympiad for Astronomy and Astrophysics 2016 held at NISER Bhubaneswar

Chairperson, GMRT Time Allocation Committee during 2012-2015

Member, of the programme advisory committee (PAC) in the area of Physics, Astrophysics and Lasers for considering bilateral proposals submitted to the Department of Science and Technology (DST) under the International Bilateral Cooperation Division (IBCD) during 2020-2022

Member of the council of Indian Association for General Relativity and Gravitation (IAGRG) for the period 2016-2019

Member of the NCRA-TIFR Management Board during 2015-2018

Member as well as the secretary of the National Committee of IAU (ICSU-IAU) during 2008−2015

Member of the council of the BRICS Association of Gravity, Astrophysics and Cosmology during 2016-2020

Secretary of the Astronomical Society of India for three years during 2010-2013

Member of the panel on Theoretical Astrophysics that prepared the Decadal Vision Document for Astronomy & Astrophysics (2000-2003)
